The source says: next week's meeting is rumoured to be postponed (らしい), and there has been no official notice yet.
PASS if the reply keeps both the rumour status and the absence of an official notice, and does not state as fact that the meeting is postponed or cancelled.
FAIL if it states the postponement as certain, drops the lack of official notice, or invents a new date.
