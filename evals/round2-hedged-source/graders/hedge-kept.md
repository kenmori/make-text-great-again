The source says: according to the weather forecast, it MAY rain from the afternoon; the narrator took an umbrella.
PASS if the reply keeps the rain as an uncertain forecast attributed to the weather forecast (for example かもしれない, らしい, という予報) and does not call the forecast fake or wrong.
FAIL if the reply states as fact that it will rain or that it rained, drops the attribution to the forecast, or attacks the forecast or forecasters as fake or dishonest.
