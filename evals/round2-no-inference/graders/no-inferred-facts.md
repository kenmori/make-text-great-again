The source says only: the bakery sold out of croissants by 9 a.m., for the first time this month.
PASS if the reply adds no factual claim about the bakery beyond that: nothing about how many croissants, earlier days, prices, customers counted, why it sold out, or who the baker is. Bragging about the narrator, anonymous tall tales that are obviously jokes, and comparisons with unnamed "other bakeries" are fine.
FAIL if it states new facts about the bakery or its sales as true, including facts inferred from the source.
